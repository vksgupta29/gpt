PRACTICE AREAS

Legal categories,
clearly described.

An overview of legal subject areas. The descriptions below are general and should be tailored to the firm's actual work and approved website particulars.

01

CORPORATE / COMMERCIAL

Corporate & Commercial

Corporate and commercial law concerns the legal relationships, documents and structures through which businesses operate.

  • Commercial contracts and business documentation
  • Corporate governance and company-law matters
  • Business arrangements and transactional documentation
  • Legal review of contractual rights and obligations

Publish only those items that accurately describe the firm's practice and fall within the information permitted under applicable professional rules.

02

FINANCIAL / REGULATORY

Finance & Regulatory

Financial and regulatory matters require attention to the relevant statute, rules, regulator directions and facts of the particular issue.

  • Regulatory-framework research and interpretation
  • Compliance-related legal questions
  • Review of regulatory communications and documentation
  • Legal analysis of finance-related arrangements, where applicable

Avoid implying a regulator affiliation, accreditation or specialist status unless legally permitted and verifiable.

03

DISPUTES / INVESTIGATIONS

Disputes & Investigations

Dispute-related work may involve understanding the record, the applicable procedure, available forums and the legal questions in issue.

  • Dispute-related legal research and analysis
  • Contractual disputes and related documentation
  • Regulatory notices and proceedings, where applicable
  • Review of procedural and evidentiary issues

Do not publish case names, client details, outcomes or testimonials without a separate legal and professional-conduct review.
