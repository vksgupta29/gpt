EDITORIAL STANDARD

Accuracy over urgency.

Legal materials can change through amendments, notifications, judicial decisions and regulator communications. Before relying on a publication, check the date, jurisdiction, status of the source and whether later developments affect the position.

Content on this site is intended for general information only. It is not a substitute for advice on a particular set of facts.

Publishing checklist
  • Include a clear publication date and, where relevant, a last-reviewed date.
  • Link to the primary legislation, judgment, notification or regulator publication.
  • Distinguish the source's wording from commentary or interpretation.
  • Check for subsequent amendments, stays, appeals or superseding directions.
  • Have substantive content reviewed by an appropriately qualified person before publication.
